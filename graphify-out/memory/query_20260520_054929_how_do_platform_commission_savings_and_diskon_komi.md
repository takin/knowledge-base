# Q: How do Platform Commission Savings and Diskon Komisi Platform fit into the fee model?

## Answer

The graph shows Platform Commission Savings as the curated/wiki-side discount program attached to Platform Commission Fee, while Diskon Komisi Platform is the raw Indonesian-source equivalent attached to Biaya Komisi Platform. Both connect to GMV Max and Growth Xtra as eligibility programs. Platform Commission Savings also connects to Dual Stacked savings. The fee model is: Compulsory Seller Fees includes Platform Commission Fee; Platform Commission Fee is reduced by Platform Commission Savings; the raw policy side mirrors this as Biaya Komisi Platform reduced by Diskon Komisi Platform.
