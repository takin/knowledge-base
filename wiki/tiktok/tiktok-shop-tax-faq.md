# TikTok Shop Tax FAQ

TikTok Shop tax handling for Indonesian sellers depends on which legal entity issues the invoice or provides the service: PT Tokopedia, TikTok Pte. Ltd., or Tokgistic Pte. Ltd.

TikTok explicitly says it cannot provide tax advice. Sellers should consult a tax advisor or the Indonesian tax authority for decisions on reporting, withholding, or crediting tax documents.

## PPh 23 and PPh 26

TikTok Pte. Ltd. and Tokgistic Pte. Ltd. are Singapore entities and do not have Indonesian NPWP. For Indonesian tax reporting, sellers can refer to tax ID information shown in Certificate of Residence (COR) and DGT-1 documents.

The source states that platform service fees charged by TikTok Pte. Ltd. and logistics fees charged by Tokgistic Pte. Ltd. to Indonesian merchants can be exempt from Indonesian Article 26 withholding tax under Singapore-Indonesia tax treaty treatment and Indonesian income tax rules, supported by DGT and COR documents.

For invoices issued by PT Tokopedia, PT Tokopedia has obtained a Surat Keterangan Bebas (SKB) for PPh 23 starting `2024-02-20`. During the validity of that SKB, platform service fees issued by PT Tokopedia can be exempt from PPh 23 withholding.

## Reimbursement for Withheld Tax

The source states there is no basis for reimbursement when the applicable withholding tax rate is `0%` or the fee is exempt under the relevant certificate or treaty documentation.

## Affiliate Tax Responsibility

The Affiliate feature connects sellers with creators so sellers can create public or targeted promotion plans. The source says creators are independent contractors providing services to sellers under seller-creator agreements, and TikTok is not a party to those agreements.

Therefore, sellers are responsible for reporting and/or withholding applicable income tax on creator commissions paid through affiliate collaborations.

## VAT / PPN

For orders from February 2024 onward, Shop Tokopedia services are provided by PT Tokopedia. As a Pengusaha Kena Pajak, PT Tokopedia collects VAT on service provision according to Indonesian tax rules and issues tax invoices based on seller tax account information.

TikTok Pte. Ltd. is appointed by Indonesia's tax authority as a PMSE VAT collector with tax identity `02.162.888.8-053.000`. TikTok Pte. Ltd. must collect and remit PMSE VAT on platform service fees when sellers meet Indonesian residency, payment, IP address, or phone-number criteria.

Tokgistic Pte. Ltd. is not appointed as a PMSE VAT collector, so the source says no PMSE VAT is collected in its billing. Sellers should refer to VAT law to determine whether self-assessed foreign-service VAT applies.

## Tax Invoices

For PT Tokopedia fees, sellers can download tax invoices together with commercial invoices in Invoice Center.

For TikTok Pte. Ltd., the source says TikTok Pte. Ltd. does not issue ordinary Indonesian tax invoices because it is not an Indonesian entity. Commercial invoices issued by TikTok Pte. Ltd. may be treated as documents equivalent to tax invoices if they include the customer name and NPWP, according to the cited Indonesian tax regulation.

Sellers should update tax information in Seller Center by the end of the month in which the information changes. If updates are late, changes may appear only in invoices issued from the following month onward.
