# TikTok Shop Affiliate Program

TikTok Shop Affiliate lets sellers connect with creators who promote products through short videos, LIVE sessions, showcases, or external affiliate links. Creators earn commission from qualifying sales, while sellers gain creator-driven reach and sales.

## Collaboration Types

TikTok Shop supports two main collaboration modes.

| Collaboration Type | Seller Control | Creator Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Collaboration | Seller adds products to the affiliate marketplace and sets commission. | Eligible creators can apply or add products to their showcase. |
| Targeted Collaboration | Seller chooses specific creators and sends an invitation. | Only invited creators can accept and promote the offered products. |

New sellers are encouraged by the source to start with Open Collaboration to learn which creators use and perform with their products. After sellers identify reliable creators, they can use Targeted Collaboration for more deliberate partnerships.

## Commission Priority

Products can be registered in more than one collaboration. When multiple commission settings apply, the source states this priority:

```text
Targeted Collaboration > Open Collaboration > Shop Collaboration
```

Example: if a product is listed in Targeted Collaboration at `15%`, Open Collaboration at `10%`, and Shop Collaboration at `5%`, a creator who received the Targeted Collaboration offer sees and receives `15%` commission.

## Open Collaboration Workflow

Open Collaboration lets sellers add all or selected products to the affiliate marketplace.

Key controls:

| Control | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Add products manually | Add specific products one by one or in batch. |
| Auto-add new products | Automatically add newly uploaded products with a general commission rate. |
| Commission percentage | Percentage paid to creators for each product sold through creator showcase or content. |
| Free samples | Enable creators to request product samples for promotional content. |
| Performance view | Track price, stock, commission, estimated commission, samples, creators added, creators posted, and product status. |

Sellers can remove products from Open Collaboration and can remove specific creators from a product's creator showcase list.

## Targeted Collaboration Workflow

Targeted Collaboration lets sellers invite chosen creators.

Main setup steps:

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Create invitation | Set invitation name, validity period, contact information, invitation text, and content preferences. |
| Select products | Add up to 100 products per invitation and set commission per product. |
| Set up samples | Offer free samples manually or automatically. |
| Select creators | Search creators, use saved creators, invite in bulk, or use auto-selection where available. |

Limits from the source:

| Limit | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Creators saved | `1,000` creators |
| Targeted collaboration invitations per 24 hours | `1,000` collaborations |
| Creators in one targeted collaboration | `50` creators |
| Products in one targeted invitation | `100` products |

## Creator Discovery and Messaging

Sellers can contact creators from Affiliate Center using creator messages.

The creator profile view can include category, location, followers, average video views in the last 30 days, and average LIVE viewers in the last 30 days. Sellers can send product cards to creators and see whether a creator has accepted targeted invitations.

Messages can be filtered by creator follower count and main category. Sellers can star, archive, or block conversations.

## External Traffic Program

The External Traffic Program lets creators and partners promote products through affiliate links outside ShopTokopedia, such as social media, websites, or apps.

Enrollment is automatic when a seller has at least one product in Open or Targeted Collaboration. Sellers can leave the program, but leaving only removes external-traffic promotion; it does not exit the regular affiliate program.

Commission rules:

| Case | Commission Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Normal external traffic | Based on Open Collaboration commission percentage. |
| Targeted Collaboration or TAP campaign | Based on that collaboration or campaign commission. |
| ShopTokopedia recommendation traffic | `50%` of the applicable collaboration or campaign commission rate. |

Orders generated before leaving the program remain commission-eligible after completion.

## Recommendations

Affiliate Center can recommend products and commission settings. Recommendation reasons include no affiliate supply, top search, most selected, best sales, and fastest-growing revenue.

For high-potential affiliate products, the source recommends three actions to receive marketing support:

| Action | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Restock | Maintain stock at or above `20` units. |
| Offer free samples | Allocate at least `3` free samples. |
| Update commission | Set commission at least `10%`. |

These recommendations are optional; seller choice remains final.
