# TikTok Shop Dynamic Commission Fee

Dynamic Commission Fee is charged by TikTok Shop by Tokopedia to Indonesian sellers based on product price. It funds basic platform-wide promotional benefits for eligible sellers, including enhanced free shipping and Bonus Cashback.

The fee applies to all successfully delivered orders. Once an order has been successfully delivered, the Dynamic Commission Fee is not refunded even if the order is later returned or refunded.

## Calculation Basis

The calculation base is product price after seller discount. Shipping fees and platform-sponsored discounts are excluded.

```text
Dynamic Commission Fee = (Item Price - Seller Discount) * Dynamic Commission Rate
```

The fee is calculated per item and then limited by the applicable cap.

## Rate Change From 18 May 2026

The May 2026 source and fee calculator show a major cap change from `2026-05-18`.

| Period | Rate Range | Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before `2026-05-18` | `4.00% - 6.00%` | `Rp40,000` per item |
| From `2026-05-18` | `3.00% - 8.00%` | `Rp650,000` per item |

The direction of change varies by category. Some categories decrease, some stay flat, and many increase. The XLSX fee breakdown has more than 1,000 category rows, so exact category-level pricing should be checked in the official calculator or PDF.

## Worked Example

The source example compares shoes and a laptop before and after 18 May 2026.

| Item | Price After Seller Discount | Before 18 May Rate | Before 18 May Final Fee | From 18 May Rate | From 18 May Final Fee |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Shoes | `Rp500,000` | `5%` | `Rp25,000` | `8%` | `Rp40,000` |
| Laptop | `Rp20,000,000` | `4%` | capped at `Rp40,000` | `4%` | capped at `Rp650,000` |

The laptop example is the clearest impact of the cap change: the formula result is `Rp800,000`, so the pre-18 May fee is limited to `Rp40,000`, while the post-18 May fee is limited to `Rp650,000`.

## Refund Rules

Dynamic Commission Fee is refundable only while the order has not been successfully delivered.

If the whole undelivered order is refunded, the Dynamic Commission Fee is fully returned. If only part of an undelivered order is refunded, the fee charged on the refunded product is returned.

After successful delivery, the fee remains charged even if the buyer later returns the item or receives a refund.
