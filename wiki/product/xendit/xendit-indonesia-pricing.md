# Xendit Indonesia Pricing

Xendit's Indonesia pricing page summarizes standard public transaction fees for receiving payments, payment links, subscriptions, transfers, refunds, and xenPlatform. The page states that listed transaction fees exclude applicable Indonesian taxes, except QRIS and ShopeePay.

## Payment Acceptance Fees

| Product | Public fee from source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Virtual Accounts | `Rp4,000` | Bank fees may be determined separately by each bank based on direct merchant-bank agreement. |
| QRIS | `0.7%` | The pricing page notes the general tax exception for QRIS. |
| Direct Debit BRI | `1.90%` | Bank fees may be determined separately by each bank. |
| Alfamart Group retail outlet | `Rp5,000` | Over-the-counter cash payments. |
| Indomaret retail outlet | `Rp5,500` | Over-the-counter cash payments. |
| Akulaku PayLater | `1.70%` | PayLater channel. |
| Atome | `5.00%` | PayLater channel. |
| Indodana | `2.30%` | PayLater channel. |
| Kredivo | `2.30%` | PayLater channel. |
| UangMe | `1.80%` | PayLater channel. |

## E-Wallet Pricing Notes

The source lists e-wallet rates with channel-specific caveats:

| Channel | Public fee from source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AstraPay | `1.50%` | Standard listed e-wallet fee. |
| JeniusPay | `2.00%` | Standard listed e-wallet fee. |
| ShopeePay | `2.00-4.00%` | Retail merchants use `2%`; gaming, content, and digital products use `4%`; both are stated as tax-inclusive. |
| OVO | Not shown as a single base rate in the captured raw text | Digital content merchants have separate rates: `2.73%` for Indonesian merchants and `3.18%` for foreign merchants. |
| LinkAja | Not shown as a single base rate in the captured raw text | Gaming, content, and digital products are `2.7%` for Indonesian merchants and `3.15%` for foreign merchants; other merchants use `1.5%`. |
| DANA | Not shown as a single base rate in the captured raw text | Transactions with PIN use `1.5%`; transactions without PIN, including recurring payments, use `3%`. |

## xenPlatform Public Fees

The Indonesia pricing page lists xenPlatform as marketplace and platform payment infrastructure for managing payments to sub-merchants, franchises, branches, vendors, or agents.

| Fee | Public fee from source | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account activity fee | `Rp25,000` | Pricing page label for account activity. |
| In-house transaction fee | `0.5%` | Fee for transactions between accounts, including transfers and platform fee charging. |
