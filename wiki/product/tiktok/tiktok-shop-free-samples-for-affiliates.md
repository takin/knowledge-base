# TikTok Shop Free Samples for Affiliates

Free Samples let sellers provide products to creators so creators can create promotional content for short video or LIVE. Samples can be enabled for both Open Collaboration and Targeted Collaboration.

## Open Collaboration Samples

For Open Collaboration, the seller must first add the product to affiliate. Then the seller can enable free samples for that product.

Configurable fields include:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Sample quantity | Number of free sample units made available to creators. |
| Request period | Free request anytime or only during a limited period. |
| Creator filters | Category, follower count, 30-day sales volume, average video views, content type, agency, and prior collaboration. |

Sellers must respond to sample requests within `7` days to avoid automatic cancellation.

## Targeted Collaboration Samples

For Targeted Collaboration, sellers can offer free samples as a benefit in the targeted invitation.

Approval modes:

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Manual review | Creator requests must be reviewed by the seller before shipping. |
| Automatic approval | Requests from invited creators are approved automatically, subject to seller confirmation and settings. |

Targeted sample settings can be edited after the invitation has been sent.

## Multi-SKU Sample Requests

Multi-SKU requests allow creators to request more than one variant from a single product listing.

Important rules:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Repeat same SKU | Same SKU can be requested again only after previous requested content has been created. |
| Manual review | Multi-SKU requests require manual review so the seller can decide which variants to send. |
| Auto approval limit | Automatic approval applies only to the first SKU request. |
| Creator obligation | Creator must post different content for each SKU received. |

The source notes that only some creators can request multiple SKUs for one product at the time of writing.

## Automatic Sample Approval

Sellers can configure automatic approval using at least one criterion and a maximum approval quota.

Criteria can include product price and creator metrics such as 30-day GMV, estimated sample posting percentage, and product categories promoted by the creator.

If a request does not meet the criteria or quota is full, it moves to manual review. The source recommends setting a large quota, up to `100`, to help promising creators collaborate faster.

## Shipping Free Samples

After approving a sample request, sellers ship samples through Seller Center like regular orders. Since `2023-10-09`, sellers use Seller Center to ship free samples; old affiliate-page shipping only applies to older requests labeled accordingly.

The Logistics Service Fee source states that free samples shipped by sellers to affiliates are charged Logistics Service Fee.

## Combining Sample Shipments

TikTok Shop can combine sample requests from the same creator and warehouse into one order to reduce shipping work and cost.

Rules from the source:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Default | Combine sample orders is enabled by default. |
| Time window | After approving one sample, the system waits `5` minutes for more approvals from the same creator. |
| Result | Approved samples can be merged into one order ID and one tracking number. |
| Scope | Applies to manual free samples only, not returnable samples or mixed requests. |

## Creator Content Requirement

Creators must create promotional content within `14` days after receiving samples. The content must include the product link.

For short video, content must remain public for at least `3` days. For LIVE, the creator must run the session for at least `10` minutes.

If a creator has not uploaded content within five days after receiving the sample, the seller can send one reminder notification.
