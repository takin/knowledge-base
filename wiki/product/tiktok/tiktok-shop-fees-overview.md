# TikTok Shop Fees Overview

TikTok Shop by Tokopedia charges Indonesian sellers several compulsory fees after successful or successfully delivered orders. The fees are deducted from final order or payout settlement and vary by seller type, product category, order type, pre-order status, logistics route, and platform program participation.

The May 2026 source set adds a major distinction: some fees are fixed or formulaic, while Platform Commission Fee and Dynamic Commission Fee are category-rate tables that sellers should verify with the official PDF or calculator before pricing products.

## Compulsory Seller Fees

| Fee | What It Covers | Key Calculation Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform Commission Fee | Platform fee for successful orders, with rates differing by product sub-category and seller type. | `(Item price - Seller discount) * Fee rate` |
| Pre-order Service Fee | Additional fee for pre-order products. | `3%` per pre-order product sold |
| Mall Service Fee | Fee for successfully processed Mall seller orders. | `1.8%` tax inclusive, capped at `Rp50,000` per product sold |
| Dynamic Commission Fee | Fee funding platform-wide seller benefits such as enhanced free shipping and Bonus Cashback. | Category-based rate, capped at `Rp40,000` before `2026-05-18` and `Rp650,000` from `2026-05-18` |
| Order Processing Fee | Fee for order processing, transaction handling, help center services, and platform tools. | Fixed `Rp1,250` per successfully delivered order |
| Logistics Service Fee | Logistics-related services and basic shipping incentives. | Based on parcel chargeable size and shipping route |

## Platform Commission Fee

Platform Commission Fee is automatically deducted after a valid order is successfully delivered and accepted. It differs between Marketplace sellers and Mall sellers and is based on the product sub-category.

The basic formula is:

```text
Platform Commission Fee = (Item price - Seller discount) * Fee rate
```

Pre-order products also incur an additional `3%` pre-order service fee per product sold on TikTok Shop and Tokopedia.

See also: [TikTok Shop Platform Commission Fee](tiktok-shop-platform-commission-fee.md).

## Mall Service Fee

Mall Service Fee applies to successfully processed Mall seller orders.

```text
Mall Service Fee = (Customer Payment - Customer Refund) * 1.8%
```

The fee is tax inclusive and capped at `Rp50,000` per product sold.

## Dynamic Commission Fee

Dynamic Commission Fee applies to all successfully delivered orders and funds platform-wide seller benefits such as enhanced free shipping and Bonus Cashback. It is not refunded after successful delivery even if the order is later returned or refunded.

From `2026-05-18`, the official calculator and fee breakdown show a broader rate range and a higher cap than the pre-18 May policy.

| Period | Dynamic Commission Rate Range | Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before `2026-05-18` | `4.00% - 6.00%` | `Rp40,000` per item |
| From `2026-05-18` | `3.00% - 8.00%` | `Rp650,000` per item |

The formula is based on item price after seller discount. Shipping and platform-sponsored discounts are excluded.

```text
Dynamic Commission Fee = (Item Price - Seller Discount) * Fee rate, subject to the period cap
```

See also: [TikTok Shop Dynamic Commission Fee](tiktok-shop-dynamic-commission-fee.md).

## Order Processing Fee

Order Processing Fee starts from `2025-08-11 00:00` Jakarta time. It is deducted after successful delivery and covers platform order processing work such as transaction processing, help center services, and platform tools.

The fee is fixed at `Rp1,250` per successfully delivered order, tax inclusive, regardless of how many products are in the order. New sellers receive the first 50 orders free through a one-time month-end refund unless another mechanism is announced.

See also: [TikTok Shop Order Processing Fee](tiktok-shop-order-processing-fee.md).

## Official Rate Tables and Calculator

The attached fee calculator is the operational source for checking indicative post-18 May fees. It asks sellers to fill in seller type, GMV Max spend-to-GMV status, GMV Max order status, Growth Xtra status, XBP/Growth Xtra charge day, category level 1-3, product price, and seller discount.

The calculator then compares estimated fees before and after 18 May and simulates commission savings across GMV Max and Growth Xtra scenarios. Its own caveat is important: results are indicative estimates only and final fees can differ.

The attached PDF rate tables provide category-level rates and final-rate scenarios. Because the category tables contain more than 1,000 category rows, the wiki summarizes policy mechanics and rate ranges; sellers should still use the official PDF/XLSX for exact category-level pricing.

## Logistics Service Fee

Logistics Service Fee starts from `2026-05-01 10:00` Jakarta time. It is charged throughout the order lifecycle for logistics-related services, delivery coordination, and basic shipping incentives.

The amount is calculated per order based on parcel chargeable size and shipping route.

## Other Fees and Adjustments

Sellers may also see non-core fees and adjustments in settlement or finance records. These include shipping-related fees, affiliate commissions, SFP service fee, chargebacks, customer service compensation, promotion adjustments, platform compensation or penalties, logistics reimbursement, warehouse service fee, platform commission adjustments, ads top-ups from settled balance, voucher funding splits, and platform or seller shipping discounts.

These items are not all compulsory seller fees, but they can affect the final payout and should be reviewed in finance statements.

## Adjacent Operating Costs

Three adjacent cost areas materially affect seller margin even when they are not part of the core fee formula:

| Area | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Affiliate commissions | Commission is paid to creators or affiliate partners when an affiliate-generated order qualifies. The actual commission depends on the seller's collaboration settings. |
| Logistics service fee | From `2026-05-01 10:00 WIB`, sellers are charged a per-order logistics service fee based on chargeable weight, route, and logistics service. |
| Tax and invoices | PT Tokopedia fees include Indonesian tax invoicing rules, while TikTok Pte. Ltd. and Tokgistic Pte. Ltd. use Singapore tax-residency documents and PMSE VAT treatment. |
