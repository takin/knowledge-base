# TikTok Shop Seller Payouts

TikTok Shop by Tokopedia sellers can withdraw money after funds enter Seller Account Balance. The source points sellers to a separate feature guide for the timing of when order funds enter balance.

## Setup Requirements

Before withdrawing funds, sellers must:

| Requirement |
| --- |
| Set a withdrawal password. |
| Add bank account information. |

## Withdrawal Methods

Sellers can withdraw manually or enable automatic withdrawals.

| Method | Frequency | Destination | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manual withdrawal | Once every `24` hours | Bank account selected manually | Free |
| Automatic withdrawal | Weekly every Wednesday | Main bank account | Free |

Manual withdrawal is available through Seller Center finance withdrawal pages on desktop or the Seller Center mobile app. Automatic withdrawal is configured under withdrawal settings and requires confirming the main bank account.

## Limits and Timing

| Field | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Minimum withdrawal | `Rp10,000` |
| Maximum withdrawal for individual sellers | `Rp150,000,000` |
| Expected bank arrival | `4-5` business days |

Processing time can vary by bank.

## Operational Notes

If a seller has automatic withdrawal enabled, they can still perform manual withdrawal, but the once-per-24-hours manual frequency still applies.

Once a manual withdrawal request is successfully submitted, it cannot be cancelled or changed.

Sellers can view withdrawal records in Seller Center under `Finance > Withdrawal` or in the Seller Center mobile app under finance records labeled transferred to bank.
